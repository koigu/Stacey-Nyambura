About me

Data-driven. Community-minded.
Always building.

My path into data analytics began in a place you might not expect: sales. Working directly with customers taught me how business really moves — through people, patterns, and the stories numbers tell. That spark led me deeper into data structures, analysis, and machine learning.

Today I'm channelling that foundation into a Data Science programme at Moringa School, sharpening the skills to build analytical solutions that actually matter on the ground — in agriculture, local business, and community infrastructure across Kenya.

What sets me apart is the combination of technical fluency and entrepreneurial thinking. I don't just analyse data — I ask what we should do about it, and then I build toward that answer.
